## Generated YlvaOS files inside the root disk

The generated root disk adds small YlvaOS integration scripts:

- `/sbin/ylva-getty`
- `/usr/bin/ylva-control`
- `/usr/bin/ylva-audio-bridge`
- `/usr/bin/ylva-start-audio`
- `/usr/bin/ylva-audio-test`
- `/usr/bin/ylva-wine-init`
- `/usr/bin/Elona`
- `/usr/bin/Desktop`
- `/usr/bin/Kernel`
- `/usr/bin/YlvaOS`
- `/usr/local/bin/ylva-desktop-session`
- `/usr/sbin/ylva-start-desktop`
- `/usr/sbin/ylva-stop-desktop`
- `/etc/asound.conf`
- `/etc/local.d/ylva-audio.start`
- `/etc/profile.d/ylvaos-audio.sh`
- `/etc/profile.d/ylvaos-terminal.sh`

These scripts are generated by `Tools/build_ylvaos_image.py` as part of this
project.
